About Our House

Built Upon the Unshakable Rock

Roca Fuerte Ministries and Academy was established with a clear mandate: to be a spiritual refuge, a place of healing, and a foundation of faith in Mission, Alton, and throughout Hidalgo County.

We are passionate about sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ, providing food relief through our community pantry, equipping leaders through our Chaplaincy Academy, and guiding youth toward their God-given destiny.

Passionate Worship

We foster an atmosphere where the Holy Spirit has full freedom to touch hearts and renew minds.

Academy & Leadership

Practical biblical education and certified chaplaincy programs in partnership with recognized pastoral networks.

Community Compassion

Faith shown in action: food pantry distributions, benevolence outreaches, and supporting families in need.

Pastors Ricardo & Susie Valverde

With hearts centered on humility, grace, and tireless dedication to the Kingdom of God, Pastors Ricardo and Susie Valverde have faithfully pastored and uplifted families across Hidalgo County.
